Amiya will be fighting the Flower Knight in an epic 1v1

She then crit again on the Fey Breeches, gaining a luck token and a lanter bloom. Her BowScab hold strong. 

Amiya must consume the flower since she have binge eating. 

The monster hit Amiya with its Venomous Sting. It manage to get to hit, but didn't connect with Amiya's flesh, so it release its venom giving her 2 bleed token and killing her.
